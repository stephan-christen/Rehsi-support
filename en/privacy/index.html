In short

Rehsi has no account, no server of its own, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. What you record in Rehsi – places, hunts, tracks, kills, follow-up searches, photos, notes, your profile and your settings – stays on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone so that a feature works

For the map, location details and weather, Rehsi sends requests to the following services. As with any internet request, they also receive your device’s IP address. Rehsi itself keeps none of it.

Apple may process requests on servers outside Switzerland, including in the USA; Apple’s privacy policy applies. Apple is also responsible for the App Store, TestFlight and any diagnostics sharing you have allowed in the iPhone’s settings.

Only when you choose

  • Sharing: sharing places creates a file you send by the channel you choose (e.g. AirDrop, WhatsApp, Signal). You see its contents before sending. It can contain names you entered, such as a dog handler’s.
  • Backup: a backup is a file you save wherever you like.
  • Field diagnostics: off by default. Only if you turn them on under Data Management does Rehsi record timings and touch points on the screen on the device, but no GPS positions, to understand problems in the field. The file only leaves your iPhone if you share it yourself. You can turn them off and delete them at any time.

Permissions

  • Location: the map, new places, and recording your track during a hunt, also with the screen locked, for as long as you keep the recording running.
  • Camera: photos of places and reading the game tag.
  • Photos: adding pictures from your library to places.

Retention and deletion

Your data is only on your iPhone and in the backups you make yourself. Deleting Rehsi deletes all data in the app. Shared files and backups are yours to manage.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) you have the right to access, correction and deletion. As Rehsi holds no data about you on servers of its own, that data is with you. Questions: [email].
