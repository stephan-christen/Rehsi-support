Rehsi – Help

Questions, problems, suggestions: stephan.christen.business.rehsisupport@gmail.com

Does Rehsi work without a signal?
Yes. Under Data Management, load the map around your places with Save Tiles Around My Places while you have a signal. Places, hunts and hunting seasons are on the iPhone anyway. Municipality, local place name and weather need a connection.
Does Rehsi work outside canton Bern?
The map covers all of Switzerland. Wildräume, hunting seasons, wildlife reserves and the game warden contacts are for canton Bern.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Under Data Management, make a backup with Back Up Data…, keep the file (e.g. in iCloud Drive) and load it on the new iPhone with Restore from Backup….
How do I share places with other hunters?
In the places list, choose Select, tick the places and tap Share. You see what is sent first. The recipient opens the file in Rehsi.
Where do hunting seasons, protected areas and contacts come from?
From swisstopo, canton Bern and the Federal Office for the Environment. Rehsi processes them automatically. The official publications always take precedence.
I found a problem.
Write to stephan.christen.business.rehsisupport@gmail.com what you did and what happened. If the app freezes or stutters, diagnostics help. They’re off until you need them: under Data Management, turn on “Record diagnostics”, use the app as before, then share the file with Share Diagnostics Log… along with your message.
